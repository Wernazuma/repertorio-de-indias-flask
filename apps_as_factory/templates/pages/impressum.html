AI declaration

In the interest of transparency, this statement records how and where generative AI was used in making this site. All AI output — except translations (see below) — was reviewed by a human editor, and responsibility for the site's content rests with the named authors. No personal or user-submitted data was or will be sent to AI providers. Last updated: 3 August 2026.

Models used

Model versions are those used at the time of writing.

  • Claude — mostly Opus 4.8, some Fable 5.
  • ChatGPT — mostly GPT-5.

Content

  • No AI has been used to alter or create any parts that suggest historical or source authenticity: no photographs, and no texts or graphics that present themselves as original historical material.
  • Some texts or paragraphs on the site have been authored or co-authored by AI — but never a text that carries a named author.
  • Authored texts are either untouched by AI or carry their own separate AI declaration.
  • AI (Claude, ChatGPT) has been used to translate text items between Spanish and English. These machine translations are not systematically reviewed by a human editor and may contain errors.

Site

  • AI has been used extensively to build the site's interface; very little of the code was written by hand.
  • Claude: routes and general code; small generic icons.
  • ChatGPT: logos for ARCA, HGIS and the Encyclopedia.

Database

  • The historical GIS reconstruction itself — the boundaries, locations, and geometries behind the maps — is manual scholarly and cartographic work. It relies on conventional GIS methods and algorithms, not on generative AI.
  • No AI has been used to create the database content itself.
  • AI (Claude) has been used sparingly to transform data directly — for example, to convert a set of places from CSV to GeoJSON.
  • AI has been used to redesign the workflow scripts that export and transform data between formats (Excel to CSV, and so on).

Data protection & privacy

This notice covers the personal data collected through the suggestion and correction forms of the Encyclopedia. On all other pages the site sets no tracking cookies and collects no personal data beyond the standard technical logs a web server keeps.

Controller

Werner Stangl — contact via the Team page.

What we store, and why

  • When you submit a suggestion or correction, we store the name and email address you enter, your IP address, and the content of your submission.
  • Purpose: to review and process your contribution, to attribute it, and to contact you about it where necessary.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by submitting the form (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in maintaining a scholarly reference resource (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where it is stored, and processors

  • Submissions are stored as entries in a private repository hosted by GitHub, Inc. (USA), which acts as our processor. They are not public.
  • Spam protection uses Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc.), which processes technical data such as your IP address and browser signals to distinguish humans from bots.
  • These providers process data on our behalf; any transfer outside the EU/EEA relies on the providers' standard safeguards (e.g. EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Sharing and retention

  • Your data is not sold, published, or shared with any other third party. It is used only to process your contribution.
  • We keep a submission until it has been reviewed and processed (incorporated or declined), after which the personal data is deleted.

Your rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your data; object to or restrict its processing; and withdraw your consent at any time — contact us via the Team page.
  • You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority.

Data reuse registry

Separately from the suggestion forms, the upload & matching workflow lets you transform your own data against the HGIS de las Indias gazetteer and download the result. When you download a finished dataset, we record who obtained it. Unlike the suggestion data above, these records are retained permanently.

What we store, and why

  • At download we store the name, institution, and email address you provide, your stated intended use, whether you chose to release the dataset (and any embargo date), and the date and time.
  • Purpose: to keep a lasting record of who uses HGIS-Indias / ARCA data and for what, so we can document scholarly reuse and guard against unattributed use or plagiarism.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting and documenting the reuse of the research data we provide (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where it is stored, and processors

  • These records — and any dataset you choose to release — are stored in a private repository hosted by GitHub, Inc. (USA), which acts as our processor. Any transfer outside the EU/EEA relies on the provider's standard safeguards (e.g. EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Sharing and retention

  • The registry is not sold, published, or shared with third parties. It is used only for the internal purpose described above.
  • Because its purpose is a lasting usage record, registry entries are kept indefinitely. You may still exercise the rights below.

Your rights

The same rights apply as above — access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction, and complaint to a supervisory authority; contact us via the Team page.
