AI declaration

In the interest of transparency, this statement records how and where generative AI was used in making this site. All AI output — except translations (see below) — was reviewed by a human editor, and responsibility for the site's content rests with the named authors. No personal or user-submitted data was or will be sent to AI providers. Last updated: 3 August 2026.

Models used

Model versions are those used at the time of writing.

  • Claude — mostly Opus 4.8, some Fable 5.
  • ChatGPT — mostly GPT-5.

Content

  • No AI has been used to alter or create any parts that suggest historical or source authenticity: no photographs, and no texts or graphics that present themselves as original historical material.
  • Some texts or paragraphs on the site have been authored or co-authored by AI — but never a text that carries a named author.
  • Authored texts are either untouched by AI or carry their own separate AI declaration.
  • AI (Claude, ChatGPT) has been used to translate text items between Spanish and English. These machine translations are not systematically reviewed by a human editor and may contain errors.

Site

  • AI has been used extensively to build the site's interface; very little of the code was written by hand.
  • Claude: routes and general code; small generic icons.
  • ChatGPT: logos for ARCA, HGIS and the Encyclopedia.

Database

  • The historical GIS reconstruction itself — the boundaries, locations, and geometries behind the maps — is manual scholarly and cartographic work. It relies on conventional GIS methods and algorithms, not on generative AI.
  • No AI has been used to create the database content itself.
  • AI (Claude) has been used sparingly to transform data directly — for example, to convert a set of places from CSV to GeoJSON.
  • AI has been used to redesign the workflow scripts that export and transform data between formats (Excel to CSV, and so on).
