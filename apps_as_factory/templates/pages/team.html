Team

The people behind ARCA.

ARCA continues the work of HGIS de las Indias, a historical GIS of the Spanish and Portuguese Americas, now hosted at Yale University.

Contact us

José Antonio Espín-Sánchez, PhD

Yale University · Economic Growth Center
Associate Professor of Economics · Strategic Lead of ARCA

José's research focuses on historical migration and social mobility in Spain and Spanish America. He is deeply invested in the idea of a digital data infrastructure for economic history; he facilitated the project's move to Yale and actively contributes to its strategic direction.

Dr. Werner Stangl

University of Graz · BORG Monsbergergasse, Graz
Private Lecturer. Principal Investigator of HGIS de las Indias; technical lead of ARCA

Werner was the principal investigator of the initial HGIS de las Indias project at the University of Graz, and is responsible for the data model, historical research, and GIS work, as well as workflow and design of ARCA. He worked on several digital humanities projects beside HGIS-Indias, including the Digital Tokugawa Lab at Yale and the TopUrbi project at the EHESS in Paris. He now is a teacher at BORG Monsbergergasse in Graz, continuing to support research in his academic field.

Paul Stangl, BSc

Blackshark AI · formerly University of Graz
Research Engineer for HGIS-Indias; Technical Advisor to ARCA

Paul worked for the project as a research engineer at the University of Graz from 2014 to 2017. He now works in the private sector while continuing to provide technical advisory support and overseeing the relocation of the server infrastructure to Yale.
