Explorative GIS

Explore places, territories and data freely on interactive maps with a time-slider. Each mapping application centers on exploring one type of items but also contains many other contextual layers. Pop-ups give more information and point you to the encyclopedia page of the place or territory.

Settlements

Focus on settlements and settlement types. Use the time-slider to explore how colonial settlements evolved over time.

Territories

Focus on colonial provinces, capitals, indigenous territories, and treaty lines. Use the time-slider to explore reforms, colonial advances and retractions.

Church

Focus on parishes, missions, diocesan territories and the presence of religious orders.

Economy and Infrastructure

Mining

Focus on places and special administrations created for mining. Explore the registered silver production by the various treasuries of New Spain and Peru.

Communications

Explore the development of land and sea mail routes and offices, the fleet and galleon convoys, the licensed trade ports, and more.

Population

Explore the demographic data so far compiled by HGIS de las Indias. This data has been collected along the route; we are most eager to incorporate datasets filling the huge gaps in coverage.

Extract

Filter and Extract

An interface designed to filter and extract records from our database. Looking for “all parishes in Chile by 1800”? This is where you go.

Urban GIS

Dive into detailed, interactive city plans of colonial capitals — streets, churches, and everyday places reconstructed from historical sources.


Colonial Mexico City

An interactive city plan of late colonial Mexico City. Mostly based on a 1793 city plan, it also includes a variety of other sources ca 1760–1808. Check out street names, churches, pulque-bars, canals, and much more.
